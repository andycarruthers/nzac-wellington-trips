*After a couple of weather-induced postponements, eight intrepid Wellington section members headed to climb Mt Tapuae-o-Uenuku. The team wrote up the trip report with each person writing a haiku for a section of the trip.*

![Road-end accommodation](https://mcusercontent.com/a8e8f7d42fa80601c5eed2fbb/images/2825375e-dfc3-4f68-811e-8687efef2732.jpg)

Adventure, ahoy!
We arrive in awe to our
Palatial road-end

Packs on, so heavy
Wetsuit socks to keep feet warm
Hodder trudge begins

![](https://mcusercontent.com/a8e8f7d42fa80601c5eed2fbb/images/6e8e1889-4653-4510-bf5f-ec8f81007381.jpg)

Up river marching,
Sun high, wet boots, heavy packs
Huts ahead, tea time!

![Hodder Huts](https://mcusercontent.com/a8e8f7d42fa80601c5eed2fbb/images/565d6747-ed8a-4e9e-abc2-2b2bebf617a0.jpg)

Dark misty torchlights
Ghostly cairn, splashing water
Whiteout, wind, get down

![](https://mcusercontent.com/a8e8f7d42fa80601c5eed2fbb/images/daca2dea-eb11-410a-af61-16c0dc565736.jpg)

Retreat from saddle
How did we find our way here
Boots off, fresh coffee

![Heading to the saddle](https://mcusercontent.com/a8e8f7d42fa80601c5eed2fbb/_compresseds/6d168c50-8c54-43ab-a5c3-819be35f4805.jpg)

On a sugar high
Downstream dead goat water, yum
Bridge in sight, job done!

![Retreat](https://mcusercontent.com/a8e8f7d42fa80601c5eed2fbb/images/2939adf5-d070-481d-a131-a0e6d5aefb36.jpg)

Drive back from the tramp,
Sheep blocking the road baaa baaa
Good food and a sleep

![Heading back to Hodder Huts](https://mcusercontent.com/a8e8f7d42fa80601c5eed2fbb/_compresseds/cce99a01-a9c3-4cb8-b654-aaa471440ca5.jpg)

Hurry up and wait
Rugby, food, potbelly stoves
Sleepy ferry rides
